✈️ Staring up at 300 identical switches on an airliner overhead panel? Stop memorizing individual buttons!

Whether you're stepping into the Fenix A320, PMDG 737, or any modern airliner in MSFS, real pilots don't flip through a 40-step checklist to find a fuel pump. They use the "Dark Cockpit" philosophy and a logical top-to-bottom "Spine" flow.

Here is your quick-reference Overhead Panel SOP Cheat Sheet:
🟢 The "Dark Cockpit" Rule: If a switch is unlit, it’s aligned, automatic, and happy. Leave it alone!
🌈 Color Cadence: Dark (normal) | Blue (temporary/APU) | White (overridden/OFF) | Amber (caution) | Red (emergency).
🧠 The Spine Layout: Fire/Hydraulics ➡️ Fuel ➡️ Electrical Heart ➡️ Air Conditioning/Bleeds ➡️ Lighting Brim.
🚫 The Big Trap: Never touch red-guarded switches like the IDG Disconnect in flight—it’s physically irreversible!
