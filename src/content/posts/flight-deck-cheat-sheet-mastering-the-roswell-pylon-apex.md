✈️ Flight Deck Cheat Sheet: Mastering the Roswell Pylon Apex

Pulling maximum Gs around the pylons in MSFS 2024 will cost you the race if you don't manage your aircraft's energy line. Bleeding airspeed or overboosting cylinder temperatures into Turn 1 is the easiest way to end up at the back of the pack.

📌 Key Takeaways:

The Late Apex Line: Roll into a 65–70° bank early and apex late on Pylon 2 to carry maximum kinetic energy onto the long desert straightaway.

Buffet & G-Load Awareness: Ease off abrupt stick pull when you hear wing buffet—exceeding critical angle of attack bleeds up to 50 knots instantly.

Thermal Management: Keep manifold pressure off the absolute stop during level runs to give cylinder heads room to cool before the next high-G climb.
